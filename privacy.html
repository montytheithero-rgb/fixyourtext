Privacy Policy

Effective Date: December 21, 2025

Summary

FixYourText.com does not require accounts, does not store any user input on our servers, and does not collect personal data intentionally.

All text processing happens in your browser using JavaScript. We do not track you, log your activity, or share your information with third parties — except when you choose to view ads from ad networks.

How Our Tools Work

All tools on FixYourText.com run entirely on your device. When you use a tool (like "Case Converter" or "Word Counter"), the text you input is processed by JavaScript running in your web browser. No data is sent to our servers, stored in a database, or transmitted to third parties.

Your browser leaves no permanent record of the text you process unless you explicitly save it to your device.

Local Storage

Some of our tools may use your browser's local storage to save optional preferences (like your last-used settings or tool history). This data:

  • Is stored only on your device
  • Is never sent to our servers
  • Can be cleared at any time in your browser settings
  • Is completely optional and does not affect tool functionality

Cookies & Third-Party Ads

Our site does not set cookies. However, when we add third-party advertisements (such as Google AdSense), those ad networks may set cookies and collect certain data about your device and browsing behavior.

We do not control how third-party ad networks collect, use, or share your information. When you view ads from these networks, those providers may:

  • Set cookies in your browser
  • Collect information about your device and location
  • Track your activity across websites
  • Use this data for ad targeting and analytics

To learn more about how Google AdSense handles data, visit: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

You can opt out of personalized ads through your browser settings or visit the Google Account privacy settings.

No Analytics or Tracking

We do not use Google Analytics, Sentry, Mixpanel, or any other analytics service. We do not track:

  • Your IP address
  • Your location
  • Your device type
  • Your browsing behavior
  • How long you use our tools

If we add analytics in the future, we will update this policy and notify users beforehand.

Questions About Your Privacy

If you have questions about your privacy or this policy, please visit our Contact Page to get in touch with us.

Children's Privacy

FixYourText.com is not intended for children under 13 years old. If you are under 13, please do not use this site without parental or guardian consent.

If we become aware that a child under 13 has used our site, we will take appropriate steps in accordance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the "Effective Date" at the top of this page. Your continued use of FixYourText.com constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Consent

By accessing and using FixYourText.com, you consent to this Privacy Policy and agree that you understand our data practices.
