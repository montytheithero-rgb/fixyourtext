Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data safe?

Yes! All tools run 100% client-side in your browser. Your data never leaves your device. We don't collect, store, or track anything.

Do I need to create an account?

No! All tools are completely free and don't require any signup, login, or account creation.

Can I use these tools offline?

Yes! Once the page loads, you can use all tools offline since they run entirely in your browser.

Is there a file size limit?

No hard limit, but very large texts (>1MB) may slow down your browser. For best performance, we recommend texts under 500KB.

What browsers are supported?

All modern browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge). We don't support Internet Explorer.
