Frequently Asked Questions

What is FixYourText used for?

FixYourText is a free online collection of text tools designed to clean, format, analyze, and transform text instantly. It helps writers, developers, students, and marketers edit text without installing software or creating an account.

Are FixYourText tools really free?

Yes. All tools on FixYourText are 100% free to use. There are no hidden fees, subscriptions, or usage limits. You can use any tool as often as you like without signing up.

Is my text data safe when using FixYourText?

Yes. All FixYourText tools run entirely in your browser. Your text is never uploaded, stored, or sent to any server. We do not collect, log, or track your content in any way.

Do I need to create an account or sign up?

No. FixYourText does not require registration, login, or account creation. Simply open a tool, paste your text, and get instant results.

Does FixYourText store or track my text?

No. FixYourText does not store, save, or analyze your text. All processing happens locally in your browser, ensuring full privacy and confidentiality.

Can I use FixYourText tools on mobile devices?

Yes. FixYourText works on all modern devices, including smartphones and tablets. The website is fully responsive and compatible with mobile browsers.

Can I use these tools offline?

Yes. After the page loads, most FixYourText tools can continue to work offline since they operate entirely client-side in your browser.

Is there a text size or character limit?

There is no strict limit, but extremely large text files (over 1MB) may slow down your browser. For best performance, we recommend keeping text under 500KB.

Which browsers are supported?

FixYourText supports all modern browsers, including Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Safari, and Microsoft Edge. Internet Explorer is not supported.

Why should I use FixYourText instead of other text tools?

FixYourText offers fast, simple, and privacy-focused text tools with no signup, no tracking, and no ads that interfere with usage. All tools are optimized for speed, usability, and accessibility.

Is FixYourText suitable for developers and professionals?

Yes. Many FixYourText tools are designed for developers, writers, SEO professionals, and data analysts who need quick text formatting, cleanup, encoding, or analysis without complex software.
