Case Converter Tool

Convert your text to different cases: UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, Sentence case, or toggle between them.

This free case converter tool instantly changes text between different letter cases. It is useful for writers, developers, and social media creators needing to standardize capitalization for titles and content. The tool supports UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, Sentence case, and inverse case options for complete formatting control. It works entirely in your browser with no signup required, no data collection, and no tracking.

How to Use:

  1. Paste or type your text in the input area
  2. Click the case type you want to convert to

  4. Click "Copy" to copy to clipboard or "Download" to save as a file

Case Types Explained:

  • UPPERCASE: ALL LETTERS IN CAPITAL
  • lowercase: all letters in lowercase
  • Title Case: Every Word Starts With Capital Letter
  • Sentence case: Only first letter and proper nouns are capitalized
  • Toggle Case: Swaps uppercase with lowercase (A→a, a→A)

Keyboard Shortcuts:
