Reverse Text Online

Reverse your text in multiple ways: reverse characters, reverse word order, or reverse line order.

This free text reversal tool reverses text character by character instantly. It is useful for puzzle creators, developers, and anyone who needs to reverse text for fun or encoding. The tool preserves all characters including spaces and punctuation while reversing the complete text order. It works entirely in your browser with no signup required, no data collection, and no tracking.

How to Use:

  1. Paste or type your text in the input area
  2. Choose how you want to reverse the text
  3. See the result instantly
  4. Click "Copy" to copy to clipboard or "Download" to save as a file

Reverse Types Explained:

  • Reverse Characters: Flips the entire text character by character
  • Reverse Words: Reverses the order of words while keeping characters in place
  • Reverse Lines: Reverses the order of lines (bottom to top)

Examples:

Input: "hello world"

  • Reverse Characters: "dlrow olleh"
  • Reverse Words: "world hello"
