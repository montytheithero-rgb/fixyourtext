Remove Line Breaks and Join Text

Join multiple lines into a single line or separate them with your choice of separator (space, comma, semicolon, etc.).

This free line break remover tool joins text lines into a single paragraph instantly. It is useful for writers, developers, and anyone reformatting text from PDFs or copy-pasted content. The tool removes all newline characters to create continuous text while preserving word content and formatting. It works entirely in your browser with no signup required, no data collection, and no tracking.

How to Use:

  1. Paste or type your multi-line text in the input area
  2. Choose how you want lines to be joined
  3. Optionally check "Preserve Paragraph Breaks" to keep double line breaks
  4. See the result instantly
  5. Click "Copy" to copy to clipboard or "Download" to save as a file

Separator Options Explained:

  • Join with Space: Lines separated by a single space
  • Join with Comma: Lines separated by a comma and space (`, `)
  • Join with Semicolon: Lines separated by a semicolon and space (`; `)
  • Join (No Separator): Lines joined directly together with no separator

Example:

Input:

hello
world
test

Output (Join with Space):

hello world test

Keyboard Shortcuts:

  • Ctrl + Enter (or Cmd + Enter): Join with space
  • Ctrl + C: Copy when output is focused
